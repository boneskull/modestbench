# BLOCK ELEMENTS
# ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Full Block:          █
Dark Shade:          ▓
Medium Shade:        ▒
Light Shade:         ░
Black Square:        ■  (CP437 FE)

Upper Half Block:    ▀
Lower Half Block:    ▄
Left Half Block:     ▌
Right Half Block:    ▐

# BOX DRAWING - SINGLE LINES
# ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Vertical:            │

Corners:
  Top-Left:          ┌
  Top-Right:         ┐
  Bottom-Left:       └
  Bottom-Right:      ┘

T-Junctions:
  Left:              ├
  Right:             ┤
  Top:               ┬
  Bottom:            ┴
  Cross:             ┼
